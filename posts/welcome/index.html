Short course description

Using practically relevant examples the students practice how to use a statistic software to perform statistical analysis and test models.

In this course the students

  1. postulate a research question using their own data or a panel data set provided by the lecturer,
  2. postulate hypotheses to test,
  3. perform statistical analysis to test their models,
  4. learn to use software for their data analysis during computer lab sessions,
  5. write a scientific presentation and
  6. present their results in a plenary session.

Intended learning outcomes (ILOs) for Programme and Course

Program Intended Learning Outcomes (ILOs) Course Intended Learning Outcomes (ILOs)
Demonstrate profound knowledge in major fields of international business After the course the students will be able to formulate a research question, derive hypotheses based on literature, identify an appropriate research design, specify and operationalize constructs, analyze data in accordance with the hypotheses, and interpret the results. They will be familiar with and able to apply various models and methods of data analysis. The students know about probability of error, and the difference between significance and effect size when assessing results.
Implement conceptual knowledge and apply problem solving ability in different business contexts The students will learn how to test conceptual frameworks using empirical data. They will be able to select the appropriate statistical procedure based on the the research problem and the characteristics of the data. Students will be able of apply the knowledge acquired in the course to draw appropriate conclusions and make informed decisions in business environments based on data.
Apply digital skills to effectively resolve work-related assignments The students will be able of conduct a thorough and systematic literature research using academic databases and scientific search engines. They will learn to use statistical software (e.g, Jamovi, SPSS, Python, R, Stata) effectively and appropriately to analyze data for various research questions.
Work effectively in intercultural teams and leverage diversity The students will work in teams to conduct a research project. They will discuss the research approach and develop a common strategy to manage the project timely and successfully. To achieve this, they will have to define milestones, allocate work packages, adhere to agreements, and reflect on their different working styles.
Behave in an ethical and sustainable manner The students will learn about ethical conduct in scientific research, with special emphasis on plagiarism, data manipulation, and the presentation and interpretation of results.

Teaching method

  • Lectures, demonstrations and practice during the class
  • Applying statistical methods in real scenario
  • Group work, going through a research project from start to finish
  • Learning tools to apply statistical concepts in real life data
  • Your score is determined by a partial exam and a paper.

Grade

  • Partial Exam (50 percent)
    • Individual written assignment
    • Partial exam covers chapter 1-4
    • Date and time to be determined: 45 Min
  • Paper (50 percent)
    • Group research project
    • Presentation of the research question and submission of the research paper
    • Pages: 8–10 pages (±1 page) per group member, main body excluding bibliography and appendix; based on the HHN guidelines for academic writing
    • Paper Submission: Friday after last session, 3 pm
    • Presentation: Last session

Note: If you withdraw (i.e., if you refuse to participate in the presentation once a topic has been assigned to you) given that you are officially registered for the course and fail. (Study and examination regulation §10a (4) and (6) as well as §12 (1).)
